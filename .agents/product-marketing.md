## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Find the bottleneck. Fix the process. Automate the rest.
**What it does:** Donovin finds where a local business is losing leads, wasting time, or running inefficiently, fixes the process, then uses AI/automation only where it creates real value.
**Category (customer's shelf):** "someone local who can fix how we handle calls, leads, follow-up and admin." Not an AI agency, chatbot, website, SEO or marketing agency.
**Type:** Founder-delivered service. **Pricing:** [Open], never shown publicly; scoped after the free look.

## Target Audience
**Companies:** Owner-operated local service businesses in Roscoe / Rockford / Stateline (home services, trades, auto, cleaning, etc.) + high-value solo operators. Niche [Open].
**Decision-maker:** The owner (also the user, champion, and buyer).
**Jobs to be done:**
- Stop losing jobs to whoever answers first.
- Get quotes, scheduling, invoices, follow-up off my nights and weekends.
- Know nothing is slipping without checking everything myself.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core:** Leads arrive while the owner is on a job; follow-up is manual and inconsistent; admin runs on the owner.
**Cost:** Lost jobs, slow cash, owner hours at night.
**Emotional tension:** Overwhelm; skepticism about AI and software sales pitches [Assumption].

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Generic AI/automation agencies: sell tech, not outcomes; remote; jargon.
**Secondary:** Off-the-shelf software (CRMs, answering apps): owner must set up and maintain it alone.
**Indirect:** Do nothing / hire office help / answering service.

## Differentiation
- Fixes the process before automating anything.
- Proof first: free look with part of the fix shown before payment.
- Local, one named person with a real phone; personally implements.
- Honest "nothing worth fixing" answer.
- Owner approves anything customers see; first replies never quote prices or commit.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "AI will sound fake / embarrass me" | Nothing pretends to be you; you approve the wording. |
| "Another sales pitch / trap" | Free look, nothing to sign; honest if nothing's worth fixing. |
| "I don't have time to learn software" | Uses what you already have; I set it up and stay reachable. |
| "What does it cost?" | Depends on the problem; you get the price before committing. |
**Anti-persona:** Businesses with no meaningful problem worth solving.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** missed calls, quotes at night, forgotten follow-ups. **Pull:** see the fix before paying, local guy.
**Habit:** "we've always done it by hand." **Anxiety:** AI weirdness, cost, setup hassle, being locked in.

## Customer Language
**Use:** jobs, calls, quotes, estimates, crews, customers, follow-up, "on a job", "at night".
**Avoid:** AI-powered, leverage, workflow, stack, agent, LLM, ecosystem, tool names, robot imagery.

## Brand Voice
First person (I, Donovin). Local, direct, plain-spoken, humble, practical, blue-collar credible. Short sentences.

## Proof Points
None yet. No testimonials, logos, stats, or case studies exist. Examples are illustrations only. Founder background (Uber live-event ops, Walgreens digital products, NIU D1 baseball, Winnebago County Highway Dept) is the only credibility.

**Business goal:** first repeatable ~$5k/month.
