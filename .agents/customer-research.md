# Customer research (Pass 2, 2026-10-01)
Sources skew vendor/marketing; treat as medium confidence. No customer quotes invented; no stats used on site.

## Confirmed evidence
- Owners use AI but don't trust it for core work; barriers = data privacy, accuracy, cost/unclear ROI, feeling less personal (Bluevine 2026 via KESQ/Stacker; SBE Council Oct 2025; Small Business Expo 2026; UPrinting via Yahoo Finance). Medium.
- <3% of callers sent to voicemail leave a message; ~27% of home-services calls go unanswered (Invoca). Medium.
- Lead qualification odds drop sharply when response takes >1 hour (HBR lead response study). Medium.
## Hypotheses (need owner interviews)
- Admin (quotes, invoices) happens at night because the owner is on jobs all day (single vendor blog: Invoice Simple).
- Owners fear setup hassle / being locked into software they must maintain.
- "Phone tag" and "on a job" are the owners' own words; validate in conversations.

What happened the last time you missed a call? Who follows up on quotes today? What software did you try and drop, and why?
